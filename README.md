## Overview

Lightweight application designed to preview **Minecraft skins** quickly and easily. It's currently in **Alpha**, so feedback and suggestions are welcome to help improve it further!


## Key Features

### Skin Search
- Fetch a Minecraft skin instantly by entering a **username**.
- Preview the skin model in real-time.

### Skin File Watcher
- **Live update**: Automatically refresh the skin preview when you save changes to your `.png` skin file.
- Compatible with any external image editing software.

### Model Controls
- **Mouse Interaction**: Rotate, zoom, and pan the model freely.
- **Model Types**: Switch between **Steve** and **Alex** models.
- **Animations**: Choose from different animations to see how the skin looks in motion.
- **Armor Preview**: Toggle various armor sets to preview how they look over your skin.

## Roadmap & Planned Features
- **Custom Skin Painting & Editing**
    Draw, paint, and edit your Minecraft skins directly inside the tool with a simple, user-friendly interface.
    
- **Further** **Mojang API Integration**
    Log in with your Minecraft account to fetch your current skin and upload new ones directly from the app.
  
- **Direct Skin Uploading**
    Seamlessly upload your freshly painted skin to your Minecraft account without leaving the app.
    
- **Drag & Drop Support**
    Quickly load `.png` skin files by dragging them into the window.
    
- **Screenshot Tool**
    Capture and save high-quality screenshots of your skin preview.
    
- **Custom Background & Lighting**
    Change the background color and adjust lighting for better skin visibility.
